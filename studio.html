Built for marketplace imagery

Make every image explain something.

Shoppers can’t hold the product, so each image answers one of their questions: how big is it, what is it made of, which version is which.

  • Feature callouts: connect product details to the relevant part of the image.
  • Detail zooms: bring materials, finishes and important features into focus.
  • Dimension graphics: communicate size with measurement lines and labels.
  • Collages: bring multiple views and details into one composition.
  • Feature highlights: combine imagery and concise copy to explain key benefits.
  • Banners and enhanced content: extend the product story into wider layouts.

Templates & creative control

A ready-made starting point.
Room to make it yours.

Choose a pre-made template for your content format, then customise the composition to suit your product and brand.

Adjust typography, colours, backgrounds, spacing and image placement. Add arrows, labels, shapes and callouts to guide attention to the details that matter.

AI-assisted or hands-on

Work with AI.
Stay in charge of the canvas.

Ask AI to compose an asset, then refine it yourself. Or build from a blank canvas or a template and call on AI when you want it.

Move elements, revise text and adjust the layout until the composition works for your product.

Localisation & formats

Carry your brand into the next market.

Every image is built in layers, so you can regenerate it for each marketplace’s image rules, each language, and desktop or mobile without starting again. Local teams review each version before it’s approved.

Review & traceability

Know what went into the final asset.

Comments, versions and approvals stay with each asset, so nobody has to guess which version was approved. Check product details, wording and design before you export.
