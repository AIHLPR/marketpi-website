Listings, prices, stock and account health in one place.

Marketplace Management is designed for sellers on Amazon and other marketplaces. Here is what it’s designed to do for sellers who connect their Amazon account.

Connection

Sellers authorise access through Amazon and can revoke it.

Listings

Prepare and publish content, check required attributes and marketplace guidelines before publishing, and compare live content with the approved version. Sellers choose automatic publishing or human approval.

Prices

Publish the seller’s prices and sale prices, and check that the live price matches. See Buy Box status and competing offers. It is designed so that sellers, not MarketPi, set their prices.

Stock

Check merchant-fulfilled and Fulfillment by Amazon stock, including lost or stuck units.

Account health

See account status and performance metrics per marketplace, with problems raised as high-priority issues.

Performance

See sales, traffic and search-term insights for eligible sellers.

AI-generated content and detected differences are suggestions to review, and sellers remain responsible for their listings, prices and compliance. Marketplaces make their own decisions, and use of the application is governed by the signed customer agreement.
