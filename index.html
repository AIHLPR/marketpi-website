You find out late.
Often from a sales drop.

Listings go wrong quietly, one marketplace and one country at a time. Then your team gets 500 warnings with no priority and spends days digging to fix each one.

  1. Approved

    The version your team signed off.

  2. Accepted

    Rejected, and nobody was told.

    A marketplace can turn down a submission without telling anyone. MarketPi records every submission and the marketplace’s response: accepted, or rejected with the reason.

  3. Live

    Live, but not what you approved.

    On Amazon, other sellers share your page and the latest edit wins. MarketPi compares every listing with your approved version each day, and you can restore it in one click.

  4. Findable

    Live, but gone from search.

    A product can stay live and still stop appearing when shoppers search for it. MarketPi flags it and tells you what to check first.

Create it once.
Keep it right everywhere.

See what’s live on every marketplace, and what to fix first.

Every day, MarketPi checks each listing in four states: approved, accepted by the marketplace, live, and findable in search. When a live page differs from what you approved, you see the two side by side, with why they differ and what to do next. After you fix it, MarketPi confirms the marketplace accepted the change and the page is live.

Issues are ranked by what’s blocking a launch or hurting a live listing, so your team knows what to fix first. You can also ask MarketPi Agent about any listing. When it can’t see something, it says so instead of guessing.
